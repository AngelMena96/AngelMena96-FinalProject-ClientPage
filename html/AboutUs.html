Our History

GreenTech Solutions was founded in 2018 by a group of engineers, environmental scientists, and entrepreneurs who shared one common goal: making sustainable technology accessible to everyday people and businesses. The founders met while collaborating on a renewable energy research project focused on reducing urban carbon emissions. Frustrated by the high cost and limited availability of clean energy solutions, they decided to create a company that combined innovation, affordability, and environmental responsibility. Starting in a small shared workspace with only a prototype solar monitoring system, the company quickly gained attention for its practical approach to green technology. By focusing on smart energy management, renewable power solutions, and community sustainability programs, GreenTech Solutions grew into a recognized leader in clean-tech innovation. Today, the company partners with homeowners, corporations, schools, and local governments to create smarter and cleaner energy systems designed for long-term environmental impact.

Our Achivements
